## Section A — The Scoring Model

### Concept
Eight factors, each scored 1–5. Total score indicates stock-leaning, real estate-leaning, or balanced.

### Factors

| # | Factor | Score 1 (Stock-Leaning) | Score 5 (RE-Leaning) |
|---|--------|------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Capital Available | Less than $20K | $50K+ for down payment |
| 2 | Risk Tolerance | Low — prefer volatility over illiquidity | High — prefer tangible assets, comfortable with leverage |
| 3 | Time Commitment Willingness | Minimal — want set-and-forget | Willing to spend 10+ hours/month |
| 4 | Income Needs | Don't need income now — growth focus | Need cash flow within 1–3 years |
| 5 | Tax Situation | Lower bracket, max tax-advantaged accounts | Higher bracket, need depreciation offsets |
| 6 | Liquidity Needs | May need access to capital quickly | Capital is locked up for 5+ years |
| 7 | Leverage Comfort | Uncomfortable with debt | Comfortable using mortgage leverage |
| 8 | Knowledge/Interest | Prefer simplicity, no RE knowledge | Enjoy property analysis, local market knowledge |

### Interpretation
- **8–18:** Strongly stock-leaning
- **19–26:** Balanced — consider both
- **27–40:** Strongly real estate-leaning

## Section B — The Decision Tree

### Concept
Flowchart with yes/no questions guiding the reader to a recommended starting point.

### Draft Decision Path
```
START
├── Do you have at least $30,000 available for investing?
│   ├── NO → Start with index fund investing; build capital
│   └── YES → Continue
│       ├── Are you willing to spend 5+ hours/month managing investments?
│       │   ├── NO → Index funds or managed REIT portfolio
│       │   └── YES → Continue
│       │       ├── Do you have strong local real estate market knowledge?
│       │       │   ├── NO → Consider stocks + REIT exposure
│       │       │   └── YES → Continue
│       │       │       ├── Is your income stable and sufficient to qualify for a mortgage?
│       │       │       │   ├── NO → Build stock portfolio; revisit RE when income stabilizes
│       │       │       │   └── YES → Real estate is viable; evaluate specific deals
│       │       │       │       ├── Do you plan to stay in your area for 5+ years?
│       │       │       │       │   ├── NO → Stocks + REITs (mobility advantage)
│       │       │       │       │   └── YES → Direct real estate + stock diversification
```

*Note: Each terminal node provides a recommended starting point, not a prescription. Readers are encouraged to combine insights from the full book.*
