## Planned Sections

Each myth follows the format defined in MYTHS_LIST.md:

1. **Myth #1: "Real estate always goes up"** — Counter: Case-Shiller 2006–2012 decline (~27%); local market stagnation examples
2. **Myth #2: "The stock market is just gambling"** — Counter: positive expected value over long periods; difference between speculation and investing
3. **Myth #3: "Renting is throwing money away"** — Counter: price-to-rent ratio analysis; opportunity cost of capital; transaction cost reality
4. **Myth #4: "You need to be rich to invest in stocks"** — Counter: fractional shares, $0 minimums, commission-free trading
5. **Myth #5: "Real estate is the safest investment"** — Counter: concentration risk, leverage risk, illiquidity risk
6. **Myth #6: "Stocks are too volatile for serious investors"** — Counter: volatility ≠ permanent loss; long-term compensation for volatility
7. **Myth #7: "Landlording is easy passive income"** — Counter: actual time commitment data; management cost reality
8. **Myth #8: "You can't lose money in an index fund over 20 years"** — Counter: Japan's Nikkei 1989–2009; sequence of returns risk; inflation erosion
9. **Myth #9: "Location, location, location is all that matters"** — Counter: purchase price, financing, management, and timing also critical
10. **Myth #10: "Timing the market is the key to stock success"** — Counter: JP Morgan missing-best-days analysis; Dalbar QAIB data
11. **Myth #11: "You should pay off your mortgage as fast as possible"** — Counter: rate comparison framework; guaranteed vs. expected returns
12. **Myth #12: "REITs are the same as owning real estate"** — Counter: stock-like volatility, no control, different tax treatment
13. **Myth #13: "You need a lot of knowledge to invest in stocks"** — Counter: index fund simplicity; knowledge barrier is for active picking
14. **Myth #14: "Real estate provides better tax benefits than stocks"** — Counter: both offer significant but different advantages; depends on individual situation
