## Attribution Notes

- Quotes marked "widely attributed" or "origin debated" should include a brief note in the text acknowledging the uncertain attribution
- All quotes should be verified against their original published source where possible
- Paraphrased quotes should be noted as such (e.g., the Nietzsche/Frankl quote for Ch. 18)
- Never fabricate or misattribute a quote
