# Personas

## Purpose
Fictional but realistic investor profiles used as recurring case studies throughout the book. Each persona represents a common archetype among the target readership (working professionals, 30–50). Personas are used to illustrate how chapter concepts apply to real-world situations.

**Note:** All personas are fictional. Any resemblance to real individuals is coincidental.

## Persona Usage Guidelines

1. **Introduce the persona** at the start of the relevant chapter section with italic narrative text
2. **Apply the chapter's concepts** to the persona's specific situation
3. **Show the analysis** — walk through the numbers, trade-offs, and considerations for that persona
4. **Do not prescribe** — present the analysis and let the reader draw conclusions
5. **Cross-reference** — when a persona appears in multiple chapters, reference the earlier appearance
6. **Keep it human** — personas should feel like real people with real constraints, not theoretical constructs
