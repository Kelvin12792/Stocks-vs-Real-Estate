## Worksheet 3: Persona Matching Quiz

*Read each profile below. Circle or highlight the one that most closely resembles your current situation. Then read the recommended chapters for your profile.*

### Profile A: The Early Builder
- **Age:** 25–35
- **Situation:** Building career, limited capital, long time horizon, high growth focus
- **Allocation lean:** 80% stocks / 20% REIT exposure
- **Priority chapters:** 2, 5, 12, 15

### Profile B: The Time-Strapped Earner
- **Age:** 30–45
- **Situation:** High income, demanding career, almost no time for active management
- **Allocation lean:** 70% stocks / 30% managed RE (REIT or syndication)
- **Priority chapters:** 8, 7, 10, 15

### Profile C: The Hands-On Investor
- **Age:** 30–50
- **Situation:** Moderate income, enjoys property work, has local market knowledge, willing to invest time
- **Allocation lean:** 40% stocks / 60% direct RE
- **Priority chapters:** 3, 7, 8, 11

### Profile D: The Tax Optimizer
- **Age:** 40–55
- **Situation:** High income bracket, needs deductions, comfortable with complexity and professional advisors
- **Allocation lean:** 50% stocks / 50% RE (depreciation-focused)
- **Priority chapters:** 9, 11, 15

### Profile E: The Cash Flow Seeker
- **Age:** 35–50
- **Situation:** Mid-career, wants passive income within 5 years, willing to build rental portfolio
- **Allocation lean:** 30% dividend stocks / 70% rental properties
- **Priority chapters:** 7, 8, 9, 11

### Profile F: The Balanced Builder
- **Age:** 30–50
- **Situation:** Moderate risk tolerance, moderate capital, wants diversification, no strong lean either way
- **Allocation lean:** 50% stocks / 30% RE / 20% bonds
- **Priority chapters:** 15, 17, 5, 6
