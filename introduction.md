## Scope

This introduction will cover:

- **The dinner party debate** — Everyone has an opinion on stocks vs. real estate; few have done the rigorous analysis
- **Why this comparison matters now** — For working professionals in their 30s–40s, capital allocation decisions carry decades of compounding consequences
- **The false binary** — The question is not "which one is better" but "which combination serves a given investor's unique profile"
- **What this book covers** — Objective, data-driven comparison across eight dimensions, behavioral psychology, myths, and actionable decision frameworks
- **What this book deliberately does not cover** — It does not cover cryptocurrency, commodities, or other asset classes; it does not provide personalized financial advice
