## Planned Sections

1. **The Core Message Restated** — There is no universally correct answer to "stocks or real estate"; the right answer depends on the individual investor's capital, goals, risk tolerance, time, knowledge, and life stage
2. **The Importance of Starting** — Imperfect action beats perfect paralysis; analysis without execution is just entertainment; the greatest risk is doing nothing while waiting for certainty
3. **Wealth Is Personal** — Investment decisions must align with values, lifestyle, and goals; wealth is a tool for living, not an end in itself; the "enough" question
4. **The Role of Continuous Education** — Markets evolve, tax laws change, personal circumstances shift; the best investors are lifelong learners; revisiting the decision framework periodically
5. **Adaptability Over Rigidity** — Life stages change the calculus; what works at 32 may not work at 48; the combined portfolio evolves over time
6. **Closing Reflection** — Wealth as a tool, not an identity; the Stoic perspective (Seneca, Epictetus) on wealth and contentment; building a legacy vs. building a number
